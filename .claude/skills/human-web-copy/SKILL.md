# Human Web Copy

## Goal

Write website copy that sounds like a small real design studio rather than AI-generated marketing text.

ICG copy should be:

- short
- specific
- calm
- confident
- human
- plain-language

Core idea:

ICG — simpler than you think.

Do not repeat this sentence everywhere.

Communicate the philosophy through simplicity.

## Avoid

Avoid phrases like:

- modern websites for businesses
- from idea to finished product
- transform your digital presence
- tailored solutions
- seamless experiences
- cutting-edge
- innovative solutions
- elevate your business
- unlock your potential

Avoid saying the same thing in:

headline
paragraph
button

## Writing rules

Prefer:

short sentences
concrete verbs
specific wording

Keep most supporting paragraphs under 2–3 lines.

Do not add copy simply to fill empty space.

Do not use random English words inside Russian copy.

Russian and English copy must be independently written.

Do not mechanically translate one language into the other.

## Example

BAD:

We create modern websites for businesses —
from an idea to a finished digital product.
You tell us about your business,
we take care of the rest.

BETTER:

Need a website?

Tell us about your business.
We’ll design and build the rest.

Another possible direction:

You run the business.
We build the website.
