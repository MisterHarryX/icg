# Visual QA — Human Design Review

Never judge design only from code.

Always visually inspect screenshots.

Review at minimum:

375px
768px
1440px

For important sections also inspect:

1920px

## For every screenshot answer

1. What does the eye see first?
2. What does the eye see second?
3. Is that hierarchy intentional?
4. Is decoration competing with content?
5. Does anything resemble a generic AI landing page?
6. Are there too many cards?
7. Are there too many rounded rectangles?
8. Are there too many gradients?
9. Is blue/violet overused?
10. Is typography doing enough work?
11. Is the composition memorable?
12. Would removing 30% of decoration improve it?
13. Is the main visual related to real ICG work?
14. Does the section feel art-directed?

After the review:
perform one refinement pass.

Do not add visual effects automatically.
